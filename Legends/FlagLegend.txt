Flags that can be enabled with the EventLoadout mod
NOT case sensitive
You can add together flags to enable several at once (e.g. itemFlags = 10010000, enables both USP and Socom suppressors)

itemFlags:
    1000: non-lethal HF Blade
   10000: suppressor on Socom
  100000: suppressor on AK
 1000000: balaclava on BDU
10000000: suppressor on USP
It is unknown what changes 1, 10 & 100 do if any.

tankerFlags:
      10: watched Olga boss fight "conflict & victory" cutscene, can skip it
     100: watched Olga boss fight "whoever you are" cutscene (resets at the start of the fight)
    1000: Olga used the lamp during the boss fight (resets at the start of the fight)
   10000: re-entered deck-E after Olga
 1000000: watched crew's lounge cutscene
10000000: watched deck-B cutscene of guard in front of window
It is unknown what changes 1 & 100000 do if any.

engineRoomFlags:
      1: west side bottom floor guard has been triggered to move (resets on room entry)
     10: engine room has already been visited, removes guard on the east side top floor
    100: east side top floor guard pushed over the edge
   1000: dead marine in locker cutscene has played
  10000: 1st laser control unit destroyed
 100000: 2nd laser control unit destroyed
1000000: 3rd laser control unit destroyed
